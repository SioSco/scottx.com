Anagramma Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

In short: Anagramma keeps your game on your own device. There's no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing you do in the game is sent to us.

What the game stores

To remember your progress, Anagramma saves the following in your browser's local storage, on the device you play on:

  • your settings, including the player name you choose (this can be anything; it doesn't need to be your real name);
  • your runs: the challenge, settings, words solved, times and hints for each one;
  • runs you've left in progress, so you can continue them later;
  • your Awards and Record statistics;
  • whether you've seen the quick guide.

This information never leaves your device. We can't see it, and we don't collect it.

What the game doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics, tracking or cookies.
  • No third-party content: the game, its fonts, sounds and word lists are all delivered from this site, and it never downloads anything from anywhere else.

Website logs

Like most websites, the server that delivers Anagramma may keep standard access logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of each request. These are used only to keep the site secure and running, not to identify you or follow you around the web.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your device, you're in control of it. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings deletes all of your Anagramma progress. We can't recover it for you, because we never had a copy.

Children

Anagramma doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Anagramma ever starts collecting information, for example through analytics, a feedback form or a mobile app release, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email anagramma.game@outlook.com.
