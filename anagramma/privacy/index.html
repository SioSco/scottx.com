Anagramma Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

In short: Anagramma keeps your game on your own device. There's no account, no advertising and no tracking of you. Anagramma counts, anonymously, that the game was opened and that runs were started and finished, with nothing that identifies you or your device. Fuller usage statistics are sent only if you switch them on in Settings, which is off by default.

What the game stores

To remember your progress, Anagramma saves the following in your browser's local storage, on the device you play on:

  • your settings, including the player name you choose (this can be anything; it doesn't need to be your real name), and whether sounds and vibration are on;
  • your runs: the challenge, settings, words solved, times and hints for each one;
  • runs you've left in progress, so you can continue them later;
  • your Awards and Record statistics;
  • your progress through the game's unlocks, including whether you've seen the quick guide;
  • the Daily's personal target you chose for the day;
  • the words from runs you restarted, kept only so your statistics still count them;
  • safety copies of this saved information, made when it can't be read or has to be converted to a newer form.

This information never leaves your device. We can't see it, and we don't collect it.

What the game doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No cookies, and no tracking of you. Anonymous counts only, unless you choose fuller statistics (see below).
  • No third-party content: the game, its fonts, sounds and word lists are all delivered from this site, and it never downloads anything from anywhere else. The only other place it ever talks to is the usage-statistics service described below, and only if you switch that on.

Anonymous counts (always on)

So that we know roughly how many people use the game, Anagramma sends a few anonymous counts to PostHog (EU Cloud, eu.i.posthog.com) whether or not you switch on the statistics below: that the game was opened, that a run was started (with the Challenge, Lexicon, Path, Passage, range, Tempo and whether it is ranked) and that a run was finished (with the Challenge, how many words, a time band, how many Hints and whether it is ranked). These carry no identifier of any kind, nothing is stored on your device for them, and nothing links one count to another or to you. Like any request to any website, they necessarily reach PostHog's servers from your network address; PostHog is set up not to keep it, and the lookup of your location from it is switched off. Counts that cannot be sent straight away are held in memory only and lost when you close the page. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, Anagramma sends none of these counts. Switching on usage statistics in Settings is still your own choice and still works.

Usage statistics (optional)

Settings has a switch, Share usage statistics, which is off. If you turn it on, Anagramma records pseudonymous usage statistics such as runs started and completed, hints used and general difficulty, each tagged with a random identifier made on your device when you turn the switch on. The identifier is not linked to your name, an account or any advertising identifier, and you can reset it in Settings. Anagramma does not record your name, email address, typed words or letters, your Seed, or any free text.

While the switch is on, the statistics are held in a queue in your browser (IndexedDB, apart from your game data) and sent, in batches, whenever you are online. They are sent to PostHog, a product-analytics service, using its EU Cloud (eu.i.posthog.com). PostHog acts on our behalf and does not use them for its own purposes. Each event carries the identifier above, the game and content version, the platform (web), and the allow-listed details of what happened, such as the Challenge, Lexicon, word length and result. Events are sent as anonymous events: no profile is built for you, and the lookup of your location from your network address is switched off. Turning the switch off deletes everything still in the queue and stops sending. Statistics already received are kept under the identifier, which you can reset in Settings; to have them deleted, email the address below with the identifier. Your Record, Awards and progress are always worked out on your device and never depend on these statistics.

Updates and offline play

After the game has loaded once, your browser keeps a copy of it so it can start and be played with no internet connection. When a newer version is available the game downloads it in the background and asks whether to restart; it never restarts during a run, and an update never changes your saved progress.

Website logs

Like most websites, the server that delivers Anagramma may keep standard access logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of each request. These are used only to keep the site secure and running, not to identify you or follow you around the web.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your device, you're in control of it. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings deletes all of your Anagramma progress. We can't recover it for you, because we never had a copy.

You can copy your progress as text from Settings (Progress, the copy icon) and paste it into another browser (the paste icon). The text goes to your clipboard only; Anagramma sends it nowhere.

Children

Anagramma doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

Before Anagramma uploads any information, for example usage statistics, a feedback form or a mobile app release, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
